When you need it

Who asks for fire watch

Most calls come from a property manager who just found out the alarm panel failed, or a contractor whose permit requires it. The fire marshal or the insurer sets the rules. We staff to what they ask for.

Alarm or sprinklers down

Panel failure, repairs, or a system being replaced. Coverage runs until it is fixed and signed off.

Hot work

Welding, cutting and roofing torches. An officer watches the area during the work and after it stops.

Construction and renovation

Job sites and buildings where life-safety systems are not live yet, or the permit says so.

Each round

What the officer does

The route is agreed with you before the first shift, so every floor, stairwell and mechanical room is checked at the interval the fire marshal set.

  1. Walk the routeHallways, stairwells, storage, kitchens, and anywhere hot work happened that day.
  2. Check exits and pathsDoors unlocked and clear, exit signs lit, nothing stacked in corridors.
  3. Log the roundTime, route and anything found, written so the fire marshal can read it.
  4. Raise the alarm if neededCall 911, alert the people inside, and meet the fire crew at the door.

Questions

Before you call

When is a fire watch required?

Usually when a fire alarm or sprinkler system is out of service, during hot work, or on construction sites. Your local fire marshal or your insurer sets the requirement and how often rounds happen.

How often do officers do rounds?

As often as the fire marshal requires for your building. We set the route and the interval with you before the first shift and log every round.

How long does it last?

Until the system is repaired and signed off, or the hot work or construction phase ends. You can book by the shift, the day or the week.

Do you share the log with the fire marshal?

The full log is yours, and it is ready whenever you or the fire marshal ask for it.

Which areas do you cover?

Northern Virginia (Loudoun, Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Prince William), Washington D.C. and Maryland. We are based in Sterling, VA.
